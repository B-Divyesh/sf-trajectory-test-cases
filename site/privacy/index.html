POL Effective August 28, 2026

Privacy.

Your traces stay on your machine. The website runs its checker locally in your browser. It does not upload fixture text, event traces, or clipboard contents.

Data we process

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, accounts, cookies, or third-party runtime scripts. The only stored value is your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage.

The npm package

The library and CLI contain no telemetry and make no network requests. Files are read only when you pass their paths to the CLI.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may retain ordinary security logs such as IP address, requested path, and user agent for operational protection. We do not combine these logs into behavioral profiles.

Your choices

Clear the site’s local storage to remove the saved theme. You can use the npm package entirely offline. Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
