## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-28

### Added

- Scrubbed tool-event recorder.
- Declarative partial-order matcher with required and forbidden actions.
- Deterministic retry-fault injection.
- Human and JSON CI reports through the `ttc` CLI.
- Local-only documentation playground with visual failure traces.
